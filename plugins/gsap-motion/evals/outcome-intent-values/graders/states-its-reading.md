PASS if the reply names the intent it read the request as (cinematic) and states the values that follow from it, not only adjectives. All four must appear: a duration, and it is in the 600–900ms band or 1s and over for a single hero beat; an ease, and it is `power3.out`, `power4.out` or `expo.out`; how many things move at once, and the answer is one at a time rather than a simultaneous group; and something that deliberately stays still.

PASS also if the reply departs from one of those bands and says why it did — a stated disagreement is a reading, which is the point.

FAIL if the reply only repeats adjectives such as "slow", "weighted" or "premium feel" without the durations and eases behind them, or if it uses an `inOut` ease on the entrance, or `back`/`elastic` anywhere in it, or if everything enters together.
